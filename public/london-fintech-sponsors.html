"Find roles" button: there's no single job board that covers every company's ATS, so each button runs a Google search scoped to that company's own domain for the exact role titles (solutions engineer / solution architect / partner engineer / presales / solutions consultant). It always reflects currently-live postings, unlike a static careers link.

B2B/Enterprise tag: Solutions Engineer, Partner Engineer and Presales roles exist almost entirely at companies selling to businesses (payments infra, banking-as-a-service, open banking APIs, core banking software, RegTech) rather than pure consumer apps. Rows tagged B2B are the better bet for these titles; Consumer rows may still occasionally hire for "Business"/"Platform" arms but are a longer shot. This is a judgment call based on each company's business model, not a guarantee of open roles.

"Verify Sponsorship" button: the Home Office doesn't offer a per-company lookup URL, so this opens the official Register of licensed sponsors: workers on GOV.UK — the only authoritative source for whether a company currently holds a Skilled Worker sponsor licence. Use Ctrl/Cmd+F on the register once it loads and search the company name. Third-party "sponsor checker" sites are not official and can be out of date.
