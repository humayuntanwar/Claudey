100 fintech companies headquartered in, or with a major engineering/commercial presence in, London.
How "Verify Sponsorship" works: the UK Home Office does not offer a per-company lookup URL, so the button opens the official Home Office Register of licensed sponsors: workers on GOV.UK. That register is the only authoritative source for whether a company currently holds a Skilled Worker sponsor licence — use your browser's find-in-page (Ctrl/Cmd+F) on the register once it loads and search the company name shown in that row. Third-party "sponsor checker" sites are not official and can be out of date or wrong.

Careers links: best-effort links to each company's main careers/jobs page. Companies redesign their sites often, so if a link 404s, search "<company name> careers" directly.
